# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for all contributors and maintainers of the Living Capability System (LCS) project, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, or nationality.

## Expected Standards

We expect all community participants to:
- Practice empathy, professionalism, and mutual respect in all interactions.
- Provide constructive, evidence-based feedback on technical proposals and pull requests.
- Respect diverse viewpoints and collaborative decision-making.
- Prioritize project security, integrity, and user safety.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behaviors include, but are not limited to:
- Harassing, derogatory, or abusive speech or conduct.
- Personal attacks or trolling.
- Publishing private personal information without consent.
- Malicious tampering with project infrastructure or security protocols.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for upholding these standards. Unacceptable behavior may be reported confidentially to the maintainer at **vellorpavan@gmail.com**. Maintainers will review and resolve reports promptly and fairly.
